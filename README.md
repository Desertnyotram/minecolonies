## About

MineColonies turns you into a governor. Place a Town Hall, hire citizens, and assign them to builders, farmers, miners, smiths, guards and dozens of other professions. They build your town from actual schematics, level up their skills, need feeding and housing, and defend the place when raiders arrive.

---

## Features

- Dozens of citizen professions with individual skill progression
- Builders construct real schematic-based structures block by block
- Full economy: food production, resource requests, warehouses and couriers
- Research tree unlocking better tools, buildings and citizen abilities
- Colony raids, guard towers and barracks for defence

## Installation

1. **Download** the JAR matching your Minecraft version from the table above.
2. **Install a mod loader** — Forge, Fabric or NeoForge for your exact game version.
3. **Place the `.jar`** into your `.minecraft/mods/` folder (create it if it is missing).
4. **Launch** Minecraft using the modded profile.
5. Verify **MineColonies** appears in the in-game mods list.

> **Tip:** if the game crashes on startup, you almost certainly mixed a Legacy JAR with a Modern
> Minecraft version, or are missing a required dependency. Check `latest.log` first.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I start a colony?**
A: Place the Town Hall block, then a Builder's Hut, and supply the builder with materials.

**Q: Why will citizens not work?**
A: Usually missing food, beds, or required materials — the Town Hall UI lists blockers.

**Q: Is it multiplayer friendly?**
A: Yes, colonies support permissions and multiple players can share one settlement.

**Q: Is it safe to add to an existing world?**
A: Yes in most cases — back the save up first, as you should with any mod addition.

**Q: Can I use it in a public modpack?**
A: Yes. Both builds are modpack-ready and require no additional setup.
